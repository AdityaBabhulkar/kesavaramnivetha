Your Wedding Logo

How to use this logo in your invitation:

1. Take a screenshot of the logo above, crop it to a square, and save it to your device.
2. Upload that image to a free image hosting site (or directly to your Vercel project assets).
3. Replace the https://mohanmeghanatemplate.vercel.app/assets/logo.jpeg links in the <head> section of your main Wedding Invitation Canvas with your new link.
